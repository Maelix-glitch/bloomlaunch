The chapters of Bloom

01 Stories

Moments that stay.

Your days leave traces — a sea morning, a song, a first quiet coffee. Stories keeps them close, for you alone. Not a feed. A keepsake.

02 Mood

Feel it. Understand it.
Grow from it.

A mood is logged in a breath — honest, unhurried. Over weeks, Bloom shows you your own climate: patterns, not judgments.

03 Trackers & Health

Daily metrics at a glance.

Six trackers, measured against goals you set. Real data only — no estimates, no filling in blanks.

04 Cycle

Your cycle, read back to you — before it happens.

Predicted dates, your current phase, how much to trust it — calculated from your own record, recalculated the moment you change it.

05 Rewards

Seedling today.
First Bloom next.

Bloom points for the days you show up, milestones worth naming. Progress, never pressure — never a shop, never a casino.

06 Coach

A quiet companion, reading only your record.

Ask, reflect, plan. Answers cite what you logged — “from your record,” never from thin air. It speaks when it helps, and stays silent when you're doing fine.
